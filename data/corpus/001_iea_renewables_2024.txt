Global renewable electricity capacity additions reached a record 507 gigawatts in 2023, according to the International Energy Agency. Solar photovoltaic alone accounted for roughly three-quarters of the increase. The agency projects that renewables will overtake coal as the largest source of global electricity generation by 2025 if current policy settings hold.
